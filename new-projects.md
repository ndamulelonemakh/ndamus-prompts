## 2. Prototype a pre-spec'd idea

```markdown
**Begin with comprehensive requirements discovery** — map out the problem space, constraints, stakeholders, and success criteria before any build work starts.  

**Design the architecture strategically**, breaking the system into cohesive, loosely coupled modules. For each module, define purpose, boundaries, interfaces, and a plan that directly targets low future maintenance effort.  

**Implement in byte-sized, testable milestones**, progressing from a minimal walking skeleton (a thin, functional slice through all layers) toward full system capability. Each milestone must be independently verifiable, demo-ready, and form a stable foundation for the next increment.  

**Optimize continuously for maintainability** — every module should be self-contained, well-documented, and easy to modify or replace without cascading changes. Treat ease of upkeep as a first-class requirement at every stage.

###
YOUR SPEC
###
```
